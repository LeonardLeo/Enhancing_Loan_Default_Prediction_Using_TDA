# Experiment 22 — Draw the persistence diagrams themselves

## In one sentence

Pick a class subset, run Ripser, plot birth vs death. Points far from the diagonal are the features that lasted.

## Who this is for

Experiments 3–21 squash diagrams into 24 numbers. This folder is the **picture** those numbers came from. Far-from-diagonal H1 points are the “loops” Experiment 6 throws away.

You do not need to read a barcode: **x = birth, y = death**, the diagonal is “died immediately”, and height above the diagonal is persistence.
