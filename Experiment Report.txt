The eight TDA folders are the live processes (split x undersample x just H0 vs both H0 and H1).
Public names are defined once in utils.TDA_PROCESS_REGISTRY. Legacy four-arm names
(Historical_Late_Split_Balanced_TDA, Early_Split_TDA, No_Undersampling,
Early_Split_TDA_And_No_Undersampling) are aliases only; they are not live root folders.

Every live TDA process runs the same five experiments (utils.ACTIVE_TDA_EXPERIMENT_NAMES):
1_PH_Default_Parameters, 2_PH_Tuned_Parameters, 6_Sampling_Ratio_Audit,
8_Null_Hypothesis_Algorithm2, 9_Revised_Snapshot_Protocol.
H0 processes slice H0 columns from the matching H0-and-H1 barcode tables. They do not run Ripser.
